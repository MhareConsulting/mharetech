The reality on the ground

A fleet that never stops changing hands.

When a few hundred {{ client_name }} drivers run different shifts and exchange vehicles through the day, the paper trail breaks down fast. By the time a question comes up — a complaint, a fuel spike, a vehicle that came back late — nobody can say for certain who had which vehicle, or where it actually went.

That uncertainty is where cost hides: extra kilometres, after-hours use, wear that no one is answerable for. The fix isn't more forms — it's a record that writes itself.

Trip history & route playback

Every trip stays on the record.

myTrack keeps the full route of every journey — the path taken, the stops made, the time spent, the distance covered. Pick any vehicle and any date and replay exactly where it went, turn by turn.

It means a vehicle's movements are never a mystery. Routine trips look routine; anything outside the normal pattern — an unexpected detour, a journey after the shift has ended — stands out on its own, without anyone having to go looking. The record simply answers the question for you.
