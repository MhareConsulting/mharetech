KasiStock · Overview

A till that was built for the kasi — not adapted for it.

Most point-of-sale systems assume a stable internet connection, a quiet retail counter, and a customer who pays once and leaves. A tavern doesn't work like that. KasiStock is built around the realities of a township venue: running tabs, returnable bottles, busy nights, and electricity you can't rely on.

Everything happens on the device in your hand. Sales, tabs, stock counts and specials all run locally and keep working when the line drops — then sync automatically the moment you're back online. No connection, no excuse, no lost sales.

  • Offline-first — keep selling straight through load-shedding
  • Open bar tabs per table or customer, settle at the end of the night
  • Scan empties and pay out bottle-return incentives automatically
  • Happy hour, bundles and Buy X Get Y applied automatically at the till
  • End-of-day cash-up, reconciliation and top-seller reports
  • 30-day free trial — no contract, no setup, bring your phone

Who it's for

Taverns, bottle stores & spaza shops.

If you sell drinks by the bottle, run a tab for regulars, take back empties, and need to know what's in stock and what came in tonight — KasiStock is built for you. It runs on a phone or tablet, needs no cashier training, and the specials apply themselves so the person at the till can't get the price wrong.

It's part of the same Mhare Tech family as myTrack and myRoutes: software we design, build and operate ourselves, shaped by how the businesses on the ground actually run their day.

Everything your bar needs

One app, from open to cash-up

Every tool the slide pack below walks through — here's what each one does for your venue.

Offline-first POS

Sell without internet and keep trading through load-shedding. Every sale is stored on the device and syncs automatically when you're back online.

Bar tab mode

Open as many tabs as you need — per table or per customer. Add drinks as the night goes on, then settle with cash or card at close. No calculator, no confusion.

Bottle return tracking

Scan the empties and KasiStock calculates the incentive payout automatically, at the rate you set per product. Full return history — no shortfall surprises at month-end.

Specials & happy hour

Set bundle pricing, happy-hour windows and Buy X Get Y once. They trigger automatically at the till — no manual discounting, no cashier training, no mistakes.

Live stock & low alerts

See stock levels per product as you sell, and get a warning before something runs out — not after. Know exactly what to reorder while you still have time.

End-of-day reports

Cash vs. card, VAT, voids and full reconciliation in one tap before you lock up — plus the top sellers that tell you what to order tomorrow.
