myRoutes · Dispatch

The algorithm proposes. Your dispatcher decides.

  • Drag-and-drop stop resequencing with instant distance recalculation
  • Move stops between vehicles without breaking the rest of the plan

myRoutes · Constraints

The solver knows your fleet as well as you do.

Tell myRoutes what each vehicle can carry, how long each driver can work, and when each customer expects delivery — and the optimiser respects every constraint without you having to check. Plans that break rules simply don't get generated.

  • Per-vehicle payload capacity enforced automatically during optimisation
  • Driver maximum hour limits baked into the schedule, not checked after
  • Customer time-window constraints: deliver between 08:00 and 10:00, guaranteed
  • Weight and volume load tracking per trip with visual load bar

myRoutes · Geocoding

Bad addresses get caught at the desk, not at the door.

Every stop address is geocoded and validated before it enters a route plan. If an address can't be resolved, the dispatcher is warned immediately — not when a driver is already standing at the wrong street corner at 08:30.

  • Automatic geocoding against South African address data on stop upload
  • Unresolvable addresses flagged before the plan is locked
  • Manual pin-drop correction for informal or incomplete addresses
  • Validated coordinates stored per customer for faster future planning

myRoutes · Analysis

Every run is a lesson. If you know where to look.

After each run, myRoutes stores the full route history and lets you step through it stop by stop. Compare the planned sequence against what actually happened, spot the patterns driving overtime and missed windows, and tighten the next plan around real data.

  • Full route history retained and searchable by date, vehicle, or plan
  • Step-by-step trip replay with timestamps at each stop
  • Planned vs actual comparison: distance, timing, and sequence deviations
  • Ongoing performance trends to identify recurring delay patterns
