myRoutes · Overview

The algorithm plans. Your dispatcher decides.

myRoutes runs a vehicle routing solver against real South African road data — not straight-line approximations — and returns a complete multi-vehicle plan that minimises total fleet distance.

Your dispatcher reviews it, adjusts what they know better than the solver, and dispatches with a single click. The optimised sequence is a starting point, not a cage.

  • Multi-stop route optimisation on real South African road data
  • Drag-and-drop stop sequencing with live distance recalculation
  • Driver dispatch with turn-by-turn navigation integration
  • Address geocoding and validation before routes are committed
  • Fleet scheduling with vehicle capacity & driver hour constraints
  • Route history and replay for post-run performance analysis

Who it's for

Any fleet that plans more than a handful of stops a day.

Distribution runs, multi-drop deliveries, field service — if sequencing stops by hand is eating your mornings and your fuel, myRoutes does the heavy lifting and hands your dispatcher a plan worth dispatching.

It's part of the same Mhare Tech family as myTrack and KasiStock: software we design, build and operate ourselves, shaped by the real operational feedback of production use.

From upload to post-run

One plan, solved before breakfast

Each capability the slide pack below walks through — here's what it does for your dispatch.

Multi-stop optimisation

A vehicle routing solver runs against the live South African road network — not straight-line guesses. Upload hundreds of stops across dozens of vehicles and get a plan that minimises total fleet distance, in seconds.

Drag-and-drop dispatch

Drag stops between vehicles or reorder a sequence and the distance totals recalculate live. When the plan is right, Lock & Dispatch pushes turn-by-turn routes to every driver in one action.

Fleet constraints

Tell myRoutes what each vehicle can carry, how long each driver can work, and when each customer expects delivery. Plans that break a rule simply don't get generated — no after-the-fact checking.

Address geocoding

Every stop is geocoded and validated before it enters a plan. Unresolvable or partial addresses are flagged at the desk — not when a driver is already standing at the wrong street corner at 08:30.

Live re-optimise

A stop gets added or dropped mid-morning — re-optimise the affected vehicles in seconds without tearing up the rest of the plan. The day stays tight even when it doesn't go to plan.

Post-run analysis

Full route history is retained and searchable. Replay any run stop by stop, compare planned against actual distance and timing, and tighten the next plan around what really happened on the road.
