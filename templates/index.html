Fleet intelligence from the road to the boardroom

myTrack ingests live GPS telemetry and turns it into actionable intelligence — from the real-time map in dispatch to the driver safety report on the manager's desk.

  • 7-day driver safety rankings with 30-day per-driver history
  • Fuel telemetry, tank capacity tracking & alert acknowledgement
  • Incident-triggered video clips & camera health monitoring
  • Hours of service compliance with colour-coded driver status
  • Shareable delivery tracking links — no customer app required

Plan once.
Dispatch confidently.

myRoutes takes the guesswork out of daily dispatch. Upload your stops, set your fleet constraints, and get optimised routes that minimise distance before the first vehicle leaves the depot.

  • Multi-stop route optimisation on real South African road data
  • Drag-and-drop stop sequencing with live distance recalculation
  • Driver dispatch with turn-by-turn navigation integration
  • Address geocoding and validation before routes are committed
  • Fleet scheduling with vehicle capacity & driver hour constraints
  • Route history and replay for post-run performance analysis

About

Built by Mhare Tech

Mhare Tech is a software development house that designs, builds, and operates its own products. We don't just deliver projects — we run what we build, which means our products are shaped by the real operational feedback of production use.

Mhare Tech is the product studio within the Mhare group. Our consulting arm, Mhare Consulting, handles strategy, advisory, and implementation work for clients across industries. The two operate in parallel: consulting informs what we build; the products demonstrate what we can deliver.
