myTrack · Safety

Turn raw telemetry into driver accountability.

myTrack scores every driver automatically — no spreadsheets, no manual log reviews. Harsh braking, acceleration events, and speeding are weighted into a clean 7-day ranking that updates daily, with 30 days of individual history behind each driver.

  • Automated weekly safety league ranked across your entire driver pool
  • 30-day per-driver event history: harsh brakes, acceleration, cornering
  • Incident flagging with timestamp, GPS coordinates, and speed at event
  • Management-ready safety reports without manual data extraction

myTrack · HOS

Compliance that runs itself. Not a clipboard exercise.

Driver hours are tracked automatically from ignition data and rendered as colour-coded status indicators that dispatchers can read at a glance. No manual logbooks, no end-of-shift paperwork, no guessing who's legal to drive.

  • Live colour-coded HOS status: green, amber, and red per driver
  • Automated duty and rest cycle tracking from ignition-on/off data
  • Violation flags before a breach — not after the inspector asks
  • Exportable HOS logs for audits and regulatory submissions
