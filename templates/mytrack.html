myTrack · Overview

One platform. Every signal your fleet sends.

From the GPS position of every vehicle to the fuel level in every tank, myTrack collects the data your operation already generates and makes it useful — in real time, not at month-end when the damage is done.

It's one place for dispatch to watch the fleet move, for managers to see who's driving safely, and for the business to know where its fuel, hours and vehicles are actually going.

  • Live GPS map with satellite, street & dark modes — SSE-powered
  • 7-day driver safety rankings with 30-day per-driver history
  • Fuel telemetry, tank capacity tracking & alert acknowledgement
  • Incident-triggered video clips & camera health monitoring
  • Hours of service compliance with colour-coded driver status
  • Shareable delivery tracking links — no customer app required

Every signal, made useful

From the road to the boardroom

Each capability the slide pack below walks through — here's what it does for your operation.

Live GPS map

Your entire fleet on one screen, updating the moment a vehicle moves — SSE-powered with sub-second latency. Geofence zones log entry and exit events, and satellite, street and dark modes suit any dispatch environment.

Driver safety rankings

Harsh braking, acceleration and speeding are weighted into a clean 7-day driver league that updates daily — with 30 days of per-driver history behind every score. No spreadsheets, no manual log reviews.

Fuel monitoring

Live tank-level telemetry shows fills, drops and consumption as they happen. Set a capacity baseline per vehicle and get an alert — with acknowledgement and resolution tracking — the moment fuel falls outside normal.

Incident video

G-force events trigger an automatic clip capture, and any camera that stops reporting is flagged before it becomes a blind spot. Footage links straight to the safety event log, so video and telemetry always match up.

HOS compliance

Driver hours are tracked automatically from ignition data and shown as colour-coded status — green, amber, red. Violations are flagged before a breach, not after the inspector asks, and HOS logs export for audits.

Delivery tracking

Generate a shareable tracking link for any delivery. The customer sees the vehicle and a live ETA in any browser — no app, no account, no friction — and the link expires automatically once the job is done.
